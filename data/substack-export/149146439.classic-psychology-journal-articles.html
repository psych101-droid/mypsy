A psychology classic, by definition, is a must-read. Yet, many groundbreaking texts within the discipline remain unexplored by a majority of students and educators. While textbook descriptions can provide a solid foundation, there's no substitute for immersing oneself in the original authors' ideas, thoughts, and findings.

With this in mind, I've put together a collection of classic articles from luminaries such as Max Wertheimer, John B. Watson, Grace Fernald, Pierre Janet, Joseph Jastrow, Carl Jung, Ivan Pavlov, Albert Bandura, William James, Solomon Asch, and Anne Anastasi. This labor of love has required significant time and effort, and I hope to expand the collection regularly.
