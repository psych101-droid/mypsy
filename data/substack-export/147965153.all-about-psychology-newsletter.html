Understanding Folie à Deux

Folie à deux, also known as shared psychotic disorder, is a rare and fascinating psychiatric syndrome where delusions are transmitted from one person to another. This condition underscores the complex interplay between individual psychology and social dynamics. In this comprehensive article, we will explore the causes, symptoms, diagnosis, and treatment of folie à deux, providing a thorough understanding of this intriguing disorder. Journal of Forensic Psychology

This scientific journal publishes a wide range of articles in the discipline of Forensic Clinical Psychology, encompassing modern trends in both clinical and experimental research. Areas of focus include Forensic Psychology Practice, Forensic Psychiatry, Forensic Child Psychology, Legal Psychology, Organizational Psychology, Forensic Neuropsychology, Police Psychology, Correctional Psychology, Paranormal Activity, Mental Illness and Violence, Chronic Criminal Behavior, Antisocial Personality Disorder, Criminal Proceedings, and Mental Disorders.

The Journal of Forensic Psychology is an open-access, peer-reviewed publication that aims to be the most comprehensive and reliable source of information on the latest developments in the field. It publishes original articles, review articles, case reports, and short communications, all of which are freely available online without any subscription barriers, thereby reaching a global audience of researchers.
