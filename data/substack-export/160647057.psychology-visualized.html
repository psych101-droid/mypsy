I'm thrilled to share something I've been quietly working on behind the scenes: a complete relaunch of my YouTube channel—and it's finally ready to explore.

Since 2008, All-About-Psychology.com has helped millions of visitors make sense of how we think, feel, and behave. With over 2,700 pages of psychology related content, I’ve long dreamed of finding a more visual, accessible way to bring this content to life.

That’s why I’m incredibly excited to be reviving and reimagining my YouTube channel.

🎥 What to Expect

The plan is simple: I’ll be using the vast library of information from All-About-Psychology.com as well as my posts here on Substack to create fascinating videos that cover the full spectrum of psychological science; each one designed to help you think deeply and learn easily across a range of topics including:

  • Mental health and well-being

  • Classic experiments and research papers

  • Optical illusions and cognitive biases

  • Historical insights and pioneers

  • Everyday psychological hacks and behavior tips

💬 Why I’m Doing This

Psychology is so much more than self-help and therapy. It’s the science of how we think, feel, and behave. And it deserves to be shared in a way that’s as dynamic and engaging as the subject itself.

That’s why I’m revamping my YouTube channel to show—not just tell—the power and beauty of psychological science.
